What RouteRest does

Know when to rest

Enter your departure, destination and target arrival. RouteRest works out every break the NHVR rules require and tells you straight away if the schedule is too tight.

Know where to stop

Each planned break is matched to a heavy-vehicle rest area on your route, with facilities and detour distance shown before you choose.

Drive the plan

Follow the truck-legal route on the map with your rest stops marked, and let the plan adjust when the journey changes.

How a journey goes

  1. 1

    Set up the journey

    Departure, stops, vehicle, fuel and the time you need to arrive.

  2. 2

    Review the plan

    See the route, the breaks you must take and the earliest arrival that respects them.

  3. 3

    Choose your stops

    Pick from suitable rest areas near each break, ranked by facilities and detour.

  4. 4

    Start driving

    Follow the route with your stops on the map and rest when the plan says so.

Built on the rules, private by design

Built on the NHVR Standard Hours

Break timing follows the National Heavy Vehicle Regulator's work and rest limits for solo and two-up driving, with Western Australia's separate scheme handled too.

Private by design

There are no accounts. Your journey, vehicle and rest plan are stored only in your browser and never sent to a server.
